The HAXXED approach

Built to hold a shape.
Not hold you hostage.

Clays for grip. Butters and oils for condition. Water-soluble bases for a cleaner reset. Each formula sits at a different point between hold and shine—so your hair decides the product, not the hype.

Match my finish

01—05 / The movement test

01 / Warm

Wake the formula.

Use a small amount. Rub it fully between your palms until the product turns soft and even.

02 / Root

Build from the roots.

Start at the back and work forward. Put control underneath the style before shaping the surface.

03 / Lift

Create the movement.

Lift with your fingers or a dryer. Let the hair find volume before you decide the final direction.

04 / Detail

Pinch the texture.

Separate the ends, sharpen the outline and add product only where the shape still needs support.

05 / Reset

Move it. Then move it again.

Rework the finish with dry hands. When the day is done, the water-soluble base washes out clean.
