Product FAQ +

How do I use define cream?

  1. Use on dry or slightly damp hair.
  2. Distribute a small amount through your palms.
  3. Smooth over the surface, then work through the ends.
  4. Air-dry for softness or blow-dry for more shape.

Who is it best for?

Dry, tired-looking hair.
