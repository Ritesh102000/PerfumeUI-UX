Product FAQ +

How do I use matte paste?

  1. Use on fully dry hair for the mattest result.
  2. Emulsify a small amount until it disappears across your palms.
  3. Push through roots for lift, then pinch sections for definition.
  4. Rework with dry hands whenever the style needs a reset.

Who is it best for?

Volume + definition.

What hold and finish can I expect?

High hold · True matte.
