India / Since 2019

Why HAXXED is here

International-quality styling. Made for India.

HAXXED began in 2019 as Dehawk Manssentials after its founder found a gap between limited local styling options and expensive imported products. The aim was simple: build high-performance grooming formulas around Indian hair, weather and everyday routines.

After 18 months of research, thousands of samples and testing across Indian climatic conditions, that idea became HAXXED—a clean, water-soluble styling range built to nourish as it shapes.
