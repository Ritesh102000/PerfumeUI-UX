Description+

Effortless Texture. Maximum Hold. Zero Weight. Creating a high-textured hairstyle without the heaviness is no longer a challenge—meet Haxxed Sculpting Clay. This advanced, water-based formula delivers extreme hold while keeping your hair lightweight, soft, and touchable. Designed for flexibility, it lets you rework your style throughout the day without compromising hold, no matter the weather. Best for people with thin hair and don't want to feel tug & pull while applying the product.

Infused with clean, scalp-friendly ingredients, it nourishes as it styles, making it ideal for daily use. Whether you have fine hair or simply prefer a product that offers strong control with a soft finish, Haxxed Sculpting Clay gives you effortless texture, long-lasting shape, and a healthy look —all without the weight or buildup.

Whether you have fine hair or want serious structure with flexibility, this sculpting clay gives you control, texture, and a clean finish that lasts all day.

Key Ingredients+

Shea Butter – Deeply moisturizes and softens hair while reducing dryness and flakiness.

Beeswax – Provides strong, flexible hold and helps lock in style without stiffness.

Kaolin Clay – Adds natural texture and volume with a dry, matte finish.

Arrowroot Powder – Absorbs excess oil and gives the hair a light, touchable feel.

Olive Oil – Nourishes the scalp and adds healthy shine without weighing hair down.

Glycerin – Draws in moisture to keep hair soft, manageable, and hydrated.

Vitamin E – A powerful antioxidant that promotes scalp health and supports stronger hair.
