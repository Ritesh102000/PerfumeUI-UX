How do I use clay pomade?

  1. Start with dry or slightly damp hair.
  2. Warm a fingertip-sized amount between both palms.
  3. Work from roots to ends, then build only where you need more hold.
  4. Use fingertips for texture or a comb for a cleaner shape.

Who is it best for?

Thick, dry hair.
