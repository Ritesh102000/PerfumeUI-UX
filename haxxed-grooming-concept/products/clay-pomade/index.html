Description+

The ultimate choice for those with thick and dry hair! Our preservative-free formula is crafted from 100% natural, high-quality ingredients including shea butter, olive oil, kaolin clay, and bentonite clay, providing unbeatable styling performance while nourishing your hair and scalp. With extreme hold, added thickness, texture, and volume, our clay pomade is also humidity and sweat resistant, ensuring a fresh and natural finish that lasts all day long.

Beeswax – Provides strong, reliable hold and structure

Kaolin & Bentonite Clay – Add volume, texture, and density to hair

Shea Butter – Moisturizes and protects against dryness

Olive Oil & Coconut Oil – Soften and condition without greasiness

Arrowroot Powder – Helps control excess oil for a balanced, matte finish
