Shipping Info

At Haxxed Grooming Co., we aim to get your order to you as quickly and smoothly as possible.

Order Processing & Dispatch

All orders are processed and dispatched within 1–2 working days after order placement.

Delivery Timeline

Once dispatched, delivery typically takes 2–5 business days, depending on your location.

We ship all orders via air, ensuring faster transit across India.

Delivery timelines may vary slightly during peak seasons, sales, or due to unforeseen courier delays.

Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders

COD orders are dispatched only after confirmation.

A confirmation message will be sent to your registered mobile number via SMS or WhatsApp.

Orders will be shipped only after you reply to confirm your COD order.

Shipping Charges

We offer FREE SHIPPING all over India — no minimum order value required.

Tracking & Shipping Updates

Once your order is shipped, you will receive tracking details via email.

You can use the tracking link to monitor your shipment status in real time.

Delivery Attempts & Address Accuracy

Please ensure your shipping address and contact details are correct at checkout.

If a delivery attempt fails due to an incorrect address or unavailability, re-shipping charges may apply.

Damaged or Lost Shipments

In rare cases of transit damage or loss, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery.

We’ll investigate with our courier partner and arrange a replacement or resolution where applicable.

Need Help with Shipping?

If you have any questions or need updates regarding your shipment:

📧 Email us at: haxxedgroomingco@gmail.com

💬 WhatsApp us via the chat box at the bottom right corner of our website

Our support team will be happy to assist you.
