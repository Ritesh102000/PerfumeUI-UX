# AURÈVE - Quiet Luxury Commerce Concept

A standalone, frontend-only showcase for a fictional luxury perfume house. The original AURÈVE implementation is preserved unchanged inside this folder.

## When a client should choose this direction

Choose AURÈVE when the product is premium, sensorial, crafted or story-led and the website must make customers feel the value before they see the price. It fits fragrance, jewellery, beauty, fashion, boutique hospitality, wine, interiors and other luxury categories.

This direction works best when:

- Brand perception matters as much as direct conversion.
- Photography and art direction can carry the experience.
- The product range is curated rather than extremely large.
- The client wants an editorial, calm and exclusive atmosphere.
- Storytelling, materials, provenance and ritual help justify a premium price.

## About the design

The visual system combines deep wine tones, warm ivory, restrained gold and oversized editorial serif typography. The full-screen opening image creates immediate atmosphere, while generous space and slow reveal motion make each section feel considered. Product cards, scent-note diagrams, the ritual story and private-consultation language support a high-touch brand position.

The interaction design includes a cinematic loader, full-screen navigation, search drawer, scroll reveals, product filters and sorting, wishlist, quick view, persistent cart and responsive mobile layouts. It demonstrates both the storytelling home page and a practical collection page.

## Why choose it

- It makes a small or premium catalogue feel more valuable.
- It creates a memorable brand world instead of a template storefront.
- It balances emotional storytelling with familiar shopping behavior.
- It gives campaigns, launches and hero products room to breathe.
- It is easy to adapt to another luxury identity through color, type, imagery and copy.

## When not to choose it

Avoid this direction when customers mainly need speed, dense comparison tables, discount messaging or thousands of utilitarian SKUs. It also depends on strong photography; weak or inconsistent product assets will reduce the effect.

## Pages and interactions

- `index.html` - cinematic brand landing page
- `shop.html` - interactive fragrance catalogue
- Responsive navigation and full-screen menu
- Search panel, product filtering and sorting
- Quick view, wishlist and local cart persistence
- Quantity controls and concept checkout
- Desktop and mobile responsive layouts

## Run independently

From this folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4174
```

Open `http://127.0.0.1:4174`.

No payment or backend service is connected. This is a client-facing UI/UX concept.
