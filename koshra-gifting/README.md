# KOSHRA Gifting House - Premium Dry-Fruit Commerce Concept

An independent 21-route UI/UX concept for a premium Indian dry-fruit business serving retail gifting and corporate programmes. It is separate from AUREVE, VOIDSTEP and RF Speciality Foods.

## When to choose this direction

Choose this design when gifting is both a product business and an operations business. The storefront supports ready-made gift sales, custom boxes, personalisation, corporate enquiries, GST details, multiple addresses and scheduled delivery without looking like a generic ecommerce theme or SaaS landing page.

The visual system uses forest green, warm ivory, almond, walnut and restrained burgundy. Rectangular product frames, hard rules, editorial serif headlines and compact procurement information create a premium but practical rhythm. Product and packaging photography carries the visual weight; cards do not rely on rounded corners, glass effects or decorative gradients.

## Complete routes

- `index.html` - homepage, gift finder, collections, bestsellers and corporate preview
- `shop.html`, `gift-boxes.html`, `collections.html` - catalogue and discovery routes
- `product.html` - gallery, specifications, personalisation, PIN and cart controls
- `build-your-box.html` - six-step saved configurator
- `corporate-gifting.html`, `corporate-quote.html` - corporate conversion and validated enquiry flow
- `cart.html`, `checkout.html` - cart, GST, address, delivery and payment placeholder flow
- `about.html`, `ingredients-quality.html`, `packaging-personalisation.html`, `delivery.html`
- `faq.html`, `contact.html`, `account.html`, `track-order.html`
- `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `shipping-returns.html`

## Functional demonstration

- Twelve structured products with Indian pricing, corporate MOQ, packaging and allergen fields
- Local cart persistence, quantity changes and corporate pricing prompts
- Six-step box builder with compartment limits, running total and saved state
- Corporate quote form with required-field validation and success reference
- PIN-code validation, delivery-date fields and message-card personalisation
- Mobile navigation, cart drawer, search, accordions, checkout and tracking states

## Production handoff

Before commercial launch, connect payment, product database, authentication, courier, address validation, email/CRM and WhatsApp services. The owner must verify all prices, pack weights, ingredients, allergens, shelf life, delivery commitments, GSTIN, FSSAI number, contact details and legal policies. Replace every labelled testimonial and case-study placeholder with approved evidence.
