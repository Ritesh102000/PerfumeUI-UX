THE INSPECTION VISIT

Your property
is the starting point.

Energy Master Solutions is mobile. There’s no office trip to arrange. Roofing inspections happen at the property, by request.

BEFORE, DURING & AFTER

Bring the questions
you already have.

You don’t need a perfect description or a folder of roof paperwork. Share what you know.

01 / BEFORE A VISIT

Tell us where to start.

Make a note of the property area and where you’ve noticed the concern. When did it first appear? Have you seen it again?

If you already know the roof’s age or have details of earlier work, keep those handy.

02 / AT THE PROPERTY

Show the concern in context.

The reason for a property visit is to look at the roof you’re asking about. Bring up the parts you don’t understand, too.

Access, the scope of the inspection and any fee should be agreed when arranging a real visit.

03 / THE CONVERSATION

Ask what the findings mean.

Which area is involved? What is visible, and what remains uncertain? If work is discussed, ask about the proposed scope, materials, price and timing before making a decision.

Three things to
write down.

The location of the concern, when you first noticed it, and what you’d like to understand.
