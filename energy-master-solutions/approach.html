ILLUSTRATIVE SEQUENCE / NOT A SPECIFIC PROPERTY
01 / THE CONCERN

A concern becomes a conversation.

A mark on the ceiling. A shingle out of place. Start with where you noticed it and when it appeared.

02 / THE VISIT

We come to the property.

Energy Master Solutions is mobile. The visit is arranged around the roof you want inspected, with access and scope agreed beforehand.

03 / THE INSPECTION

The roof comes into focus.

Look at the covering, edges and connections. Ask what is visible and what still needs to be understood.

04 / THE CONVERSATION

You understand the options.

If work is proposed, ask about the area involved, the materials, the scope and the cost before you decide.

01
BEFORE A VISIT

TELL US
WHERE TO START.

Make a note of the property area, the part of the home involved, and when you first noticed the change.

If you know the roof’s age or have details of previous work, keep those handy. Confirm the service area, access, scope and any fee when arranging a real visit.

02
AT THE PROPERTY

PUT THE CONCERN
IN CONTEXT.

Point out what you have noticed and bring up the questions you do not yet know how to answer.

Discuss which areas can be accessed and what the inspection can establish. Some parts of a roof may not be visible; ask what remains uncertain.

03
AFTER THE LOOK

UNDERSTAND
WHAT COMES NEXT.

Ask what the observations mean for your property. Which area is involved? What needs a closer look?

If work is proposed, ask for the scope, materials, price inclusions and timing. Get those details clear before making a decision.

A LITTLE PREPARATION HELPS

BRING YOUR
QUESTIONS.

A few notes are enough. You do not need a roofing vocabulary or a complete history of the house.
