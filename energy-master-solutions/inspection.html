Where does the inspection happen?+

At your property. Energy Master Solutions operates as a mobile roofing business.

What should I include in my request?+

Share your property area, your contact details, and a short description of your concern. Mention when you first noticed it if you know.

Do I need to know whether I need repairs or replacement?+

No. Start with what you have noticed. The inspection is the starting point for discussing your roof.
