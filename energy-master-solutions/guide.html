HOMEOWNER QUESTIONS

Know what to ask.
Take your time deciding.

You should not need a roofing vocabulary to start a conversation. These answers and prompts help you prepare.

Download question sheet
Do I need to know whether it needs repair or replacement?+

No. Describe what you’ve noticed first. The condition of the property needs to be understood before that discussion.

Do I need to visit an office?+

No. Energy Master Solutions is a mobile roofing company. Inspections take place at the property, by request.

What should I mention when I ask for an inspection?+

The property’s area, where you have noticed a change, and when it started. Add the roof’s age or past repairs if you already know them.

Can a stain be diagnosed from this website?+

No. This website helps you describe a concern. A mark by itself does not establish where the water came from.

Does this form book an appointment?+

No. This demo prepares a request preview. Nothing is sent to the company, stored by the website, or booked.

Do you cover my area?+

The exact service area is still to be confirmed. Entering an area in the demo does not confirm availability.

Is there an inspection fee?+

Pricing has not been supplied for this concept. Confirm any fee before arranging a real visit.

Are the gallery images completed company projects?+

The photographs are frames from supplied roofing reference footage. The drawings are illustrations. Neither is presented as verified Energy Master Solutions project documentation.
