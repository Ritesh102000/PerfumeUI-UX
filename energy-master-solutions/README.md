# Energy Master Solutions

Static five-page mobile roofing demo at https://websiteshowcase.catapultaiwork.com/energy-master-solutions/.

The roof assembly starts muted on page load and loops, except when reduced motion is requested. Pause, layer selection and timeline seeking remain under visitor control.

The inspection form validates and previews only; it does not submit or store personal details. Contact details and the precise service area are pending. The footage is supplied reference material rather than a verified company portfolio.

Editable media and Remotion source are maintained in the local Energy Master Solutions project. Fonts are self-hosted; their licenses accompany the deployment.
