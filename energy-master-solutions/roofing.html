01 / YOUR ROOF

WHAT HAVE
YOU NOTICED?

You don’t have to decide whether it needs repair or replacement. Tell us what has changed at your property.

START WITH WHAT YOU SEE

A few ordinary observations.
A useful place to begin.

Describe what you can see from a safe position. You do not need to climb onto the roof.

01 / Water stain or leak

Water where it shouldn’t be.

A new ceiling mark. A damp patch near a window. A drip that appears when it rains. Start with the place you noticed it.

A mark inside is a clue, not a diagnosis. The conversation needs to connect what you can see with the roof above it.

A USEFUL FIRST NOTE

Where does it appear? Is it constant, or does it follow rain?

Start with this concern
02 / Visible shingle damage

Something looks out of place.

A missing piece, a lifted edge, or an area that looks different from the rest. Describe the side of the roof you can see.

The visible covering is one part of a roof system. A property inspection helps put the area you are concerned about in context.

A USEFUL FIRST NOTE

Which side of the property? When did you first notice the change?

Start with this concern
03 / Change after rough weather

A change after the weather.

Maybe you noticed something after wind or rain. You do not need to decide what caused it before asking about a visit.

Keep the description specific: what changed, where you saw it, and when. Those details give the conversation a useful starting point.

A USEFUL FIRST NOTE

What changed? Do you know when the weather happened?

Start with this concern
04 / General inspection

A roof you’d like to understand.

You may not know its age, what was done before, or where to begin. “I would like the roof checked” is a perfectly useful start.

Bring the questions you already have. If you know about previous work, keep those details nearby; there is no need to guess.

A USEFUL FIRST NOTE

What would you like explained? Do you know of any earlier work?

Start with this concern
